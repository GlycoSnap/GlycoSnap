About Us

GlycoSnap utilizes cutting-edge computer vision technology to assess the glycemic load of food portions. By simply taking or uploading a photo of their plate, GlycoSnap seamlessly analyzes visual data and users receive an accurate glycemic load calculation, helping them make informed dietary choices, promoting healthier lifestyles and improved well-being.

GlycoSnap Features

AI-assisted Glycemic Load Estimation

With the use of AI techniques such as computer vision and image analysis, GlycoSnap is able to predict the Glycemic Load of any food portion.

Dietary
Recommendations

How it works

GlycoSnap is incredibly user-friendly, making it easy for you to track your diet and nutrition with just a few taps.

Upload a picture

Simply snap or upload a photo of your plate, and our intelligent image recognition technology will analyze the content.

Glycemic load information

GlycoSnap automatically identifies the foods and provides you with their glycemic load information

Store your meals

Store your meal history and receive comprehensive weekly reviews to help you stay on track with your dietary goals.
